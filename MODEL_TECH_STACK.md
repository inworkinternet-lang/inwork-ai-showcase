# 🧠 InWork-Ai: Model Tech Stack Manifest

## 🏛️ Primary Intelligence Layer
Our system is designed with a **Hybrid Model Strategy**, selecting the best engine for each specific task in the production pipeline.

### 🏆 GLM (Zhipu AI) — The "System Architect"
**Models:** `glm-4-plus`, `glm-5.1`
**Role:** Primary Backend Intelligence & Data Structuralist.
-   **Task 1:** Structural code generation and microservice scaffolding.
-   **Task 2:** Deep context analysis of business "tenants" (SSOT validation).
-   **Task 3:** Post-call analytical processing (Extracting JSON from long-form voice interactions).

### ⚡ Cerebras / Groq — The "Response Engines"
**Models:** `llama-3.1-70b/8b` (Inference via Hardware Accelerators)
**Role:** Real-time Voice Interaction.
-   **Task:** Sub-100ms response generation during live customer calls.

---

## ❓ Why We Use GLM?
We chose **GLM** as our structural foundation because of its superior reasoning capabilities and excellent handling of complex JSON structures. Integrating GLM has allowed us to:
1.  Maintain a strict **Flat Columns Strategy** in our databases.
2.  Automate the generation of **Smart-Link** logic based on multi-step business requirements.
3.  Ensure high compatibility with modern agentic frameworks (OpenCode, KiloCode).

---

## 🚀 Future with MiMo-V2.5
The addition of **MiMo-V2.5** into our stack will complement our existing GLM-based infrastructure. While GLM acts as the "Architect" for structured tasks, MiMo will serve as the **"Infinite Memory"** for our agents, allowing them to reference months of business history during a single interaction.
